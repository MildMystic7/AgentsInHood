# Code of Conduct

AgentsInHood should be a rigorous and welcoming place to build, question, and learn.

## Our standards

We expect contributors to:

- discuss ideas and evidence respectfully;
- give actionable feedback and assume good faith;
- disclose relevant limitations, conflicts, and uncertainty;
- protect other people's privacy and project credentials;
- credit the work of others; and
- accept moderation decisions made to keep the community safe and productive.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, impersonation, spam, market manipulation, and
deliberately misleading performance claims are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when behavior
violates these standards. Serious or repeated incidents may lead to a permanent ban.

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through GitHub. Do not publish sensitive
details in an issue. Reports will be reviewed as promptly and confidentially as practical.

This code applies in the repository, Discussions, project social spaces, and private exchanges
made in an official project capacity.
